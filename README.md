## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Our proposed solution is to create a digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with customers and bulk buyers. Farmers can register on the website and upload their product details with prices. Customers can view the available products, compare prices and place orders easily. This helps farmers sell their products without depending on many middlemen.

The platform will also provide delivery support to transport products from farmers to customers. AI can be used to predict product demand and suggest better delivery routes. Through this system, farmers can get better prices for their products, and customers can buy fresh products at reasonable prices.

## Technical Approach
We plan to develop this project using HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the frontend and Python with Django for the backend. MySQL or SQLite can be used to store farmer details, customer information, product details and orders. The website will have separate login options for farmers and customers.
Farmers can add their products, and customers can view and order them through the website. AI can be used to predict product demand and Google Maps can help to find suitable delivery routes. The main aim is to make the website simple and easy for everyone to use.

## Feasibility and Viability
This project is possible to develop using commonly available technologies. We can use open-source tools like Python and Django, which help to reduce the development cost. Farmers and customers can access the website using mobile phones or computers. Some farmers may find it difficult to use the website, and internet facilities may not be available in all villages. Delivery and transportation may also be challenging. These problems can be reduced by creating a simple website and providing proper delivery support. Strategies include a simple interface with regional language support, a low-data mobile-friendly design, and tie-ups with local delivery partners. 

## Impact and Benefits
This project will be helpful for both farmers and customers. Farmers can sell their products directly without depending on many middlemen. They can get better prices and reach more customers. Customers can buy fresh agricultural products at reasonable prices. The platform can also reduce food wastage and unnecessary transportation costs. Overall, it helps to improve farmers' income and makes the selling and buying process easier. Better route planning reduces fuel use, and better demand prediction helps reduce food wastage.
